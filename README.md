# 🚀 Kickstarter Projects Performance & Analytics Dashboard

An interactive, multi-page Power BI analytics dashboard analyzing over 375,000 Kickstarter campaign projects. The dashboard evaluates overall platform performance, successful vs. failed project characteristics, funding goals, pledged amounts, and backer behavior across different regions and categories.

## 🔑 Key Platform Metrics & KPIs
- Total Projects: 375K
- Total Backers: 40M
- Total Funding Goal: $17Bn
- Total Amount Pledged: $3Bn
- Successful Projects: 134K
- Failed Projects: 198K

---

## 📈 Key Insights & Dashboard Highlights

### 📊 Overall Performance
- Top Project by Pledged: *Pebble Time - Awesome Smartwatch* lead the highest pledged funding, followed by *Coolest Cooler*.
- Geographic Dominance: The United States accounts for the vast majority of projects (~0.29M), followed by the United Kingdom and Canada.
- Historical Growth: Project counts grew steadily from 2009, peaking around 2014–2015 before stabilizing.

### ✅ Successful vs. ❌ Failed Projects Comparison
- Successful Campaigns (134K): Driven by strong categories like Film & Video, Music, and Publishing. They usually feature realistic funding goals ($1Bn total goal vs $3Bn total pledged achieved).
- Failed Campaigns (198K): Characterized by unrealistically high funding goals ($12Bn total combined goal vs only $261M pledged). Major failed categories include Film & Video, Publishing, and Technology.
- Decomposition Tree Analysis: Breakdown pathways across Country ➡️ Category ➡️ Subcategory to pinpoint top contributing segments for success and failure.

### 👥 Backer Engagement Analysis
- Top Categories by Backers: Games (11.3M backers) and Design (7.3M backers) attract the highest community engagement.
- Top Subcategories: *Product Design* and *Tabletop Games* dominate total backer counts.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- Business Intelligence & Analytics: Microsoft Power BI
- Data Transformation & Preparation: Power Query (ETL process)
- Advanced Calculations & Measures: DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Visualizations Used: Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), Line Charts, Horizontal Bar Charts, Treemaps, Donut Charts, Decomposition Trees, and Map Visuals.
